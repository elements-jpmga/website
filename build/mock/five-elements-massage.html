About the treatment

Body and mind, treated together

In Traditional Chinese Medicine, the body and mind are never treated separately. Each of the five elements governs not just a set of organs, but a mood, a tension pattern and a way of moving through the world. When one element is out of balance, you feel it — in your muscles, your sleep, your energy and your emotional steadiness. 5 Elements Massage works with that connection, offering holistic relaxation and recovery shaped around where you are today.

Your element shapes the whole session

The element you choose sets every part of the treatment — the oil blend selected for your state, the technique and pressure of your massage, and the enhancement that completes it. Nothing is generic.

A little more clarity

Your questions answered

Do I choose my own element, or does the therapist choose for me?

You choose. If you're unsure which element fits how you're feeling today, your therapist is happy to talk it through with you — but the decision is always yours.

What if how I feel fits more than one element?

Choose the one that describes your most pressing concern today. There's no wrong answer and no fixed element — the series gives you the freedom to explore a different element on your next visit.

What are the Five Elements in TCM?

They are five linked parts of TCM theory — Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water. Each is associated with particular organs, a mood and a pattern of tension: Wood, for example, with the liver, irritability and knots in the neck and upper back. When one element feels out of balance, you notice it in your muscles, sleep, energy or mood. In this massage, the elements help you match the session to how you feel.

What makes the 5 Elements Massage different?

Every session combines a customised oil blend, a matched enhancement and skilled full-body bodywork — all organised around how your body feels and how you feel. Because you choose your element each visit, the treatment changes as you do.

How often should I have a 5 Elements Massage?

For maintenance: once or twice a month. You can choose a different element each time, depending on how you feel.

Will cupping leave marks?

If you choose Metal, cupping can leave round, reddish marks where the cups were placed. They are temporary and usually fade within one to two weeks. You may also feel a little tender in those areas afterwards.
