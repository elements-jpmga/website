About the treatment

Why you feel heavy and puffy

Long hours at a desk, standing all day, flights and hard training can leave fluid sitting in your tissues. You notice it as puffiness, bloating or heaviness — or a sense that you aren’t bouncing back, even when you’re doing all the “right” things.

Your lymphatic system is the network of vessels and nodes that collects this excess fluid and waste from your tissues and returns it to your circulation. Unlike your blood, lymph has no heart to pump it — it relies on muscle movement, breathing and gentle pressure changes to keep flowing. When it becomes sluggish, you may find that:

  • your body’s natural detox feels less efficient, so recovery seems slow and inflammation may linger
  • your immune defences may feel less responsive, and other wellness therapies may seem to do less for you
  • swelling and puffiness hang around, making movement — and other treatments — feel less effective
  • recovery after workouts, injuries or surgery may take longer than you would like
  • soreness and fatigue last, slowing your progress if you train or exercise regularly

Encouraging lymph flow can help your body reset and recover, so you get more from every other treatment and healthy habit you invest in.

How lymphatic drainage works

Lymphatic drainage uses light, rhythmic strokes that follow the natural direction of lymph flow, moving excess fluid and waste from your tissues towards your lymph nodes. The pressure stays light on purpose — the smallest lymph vessels lie in the skin and open with a gentle stretch, so the strokes don’t need to press deep to move fluid. The result supports your body’s natural detox and helps reduce the appearance of puffiness and water retention.

Lymphatic brushing first

Every session begins with lymphatic brush activation. Gentle brushing over your body stimulates your lymph nodes and awakens your meridians — the energy channels of Traditional Chinese Medicine — preparing your body for the massage that follows. If you would rather go straight to the massage, you can skip it.

Detox Aromatherapy Oil

The massage uses our Detox Aromatherapy Oil, a refreshing citrus blend of bergamot, grapefruit and orange essential oils. It is formulated to purify and support lymphatic flow, and its bright citrus scent adds to the sense of feeling refreshed.

Classic or TCM-inspired — your choice

Every session is tailored to you. Classic lymphatic strokes are gentle, slow and relaxing. TCM-inspired techniques are firmer, for when you prefer to feel more pressure. Our therapists are highly experienced and responsive to your preferences, so your comfort always comes first.

Your questions answered

What is lymphatic drainage massage?

It is a gentle, rhythmic massage that encourages lymph — the clear fluid that collects in your tissues — to move towards your lymph nodes. Your therapist uses light strokes that follow the direction of lymph flow, which helps reduce the appearance of puffiness and leaves you feeling lighter.

Is lymphatic drainage massage painful?

No — the technique is gentle and relaxing. If you prefer more pressure, TCM-inspired techniques can be firmer.

What is the difference between classic and TCM-inspired techniques?

Classic lymphatic strokes are light, slow and rhythmic. TCM-inspired techniques draw on Traditional Chinese Medicine and use firmer pressure. You choose the style before your session, and you can tell your therapist if you want the pressure changed.

What is lymphatic brush activation — and can I skip it?

It is gentle brushing over your body at the start of the session, which stimulates your lymph nodes and awakens your meridians before the massage. It is part of every session, but you can skip it if you prefer.

Which areas are massaged?

The massage may cover your neck, collarbone, arms, abdomen, legs and back. Let your therapist know which areas you want to focus on.

Do I need to prepare before the treatment?

Just come as you are. Drinking water before and after your session helps support your body’s natural detox.

How often should I have lymphatic drainage massage?

For maintenance: Once a month.
