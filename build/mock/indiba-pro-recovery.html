About the treatment

How INDIBA®
works

448 kHz

Proionic® current

Technology and skilled touch

While traditional radiofrequency relies primarily on active thermal heat to boost collagen, INDIBA® adds a deeper physiological dimension. Powered by a patented 448 kHz Proionic® current, it supports cell function and faster tissue recovery, even with only gentle warmth. Adaptable for both face and body, each session works across superficial and deeper layers, with the focus set by the treatment you choose.

Trusted in professional sport

INDIBA® technology is used by professional sports teams, hospitals and clinics across Europe — including Rafael Nadal, FC Barcelona, the French Tennis Federation and several national teams at the London 2012 Olympics. It is backed by multiple medical studies.

Warmth that reaches deep tissue

INDIBA® raises the temperature of the tissue from within. That warmth brings more oxygen and nutrients to the area, supporting the body's own tissue regeneration. It reaches deeper, more resistant tissue — joints, ligaments and tendons — helping repair them while easing pain.

Technology and skilled touch

Your therapist works with the INDIBA® handpiece and with their hands. This hands-on method directs the treatment precisely to where you feel tight or sore, combining the technology with a therapist's skilled touch.

Overuse and everyday strain

Repeated training, long runs and hours in one posture leave tiny tears in connective tissue. INDIBA® supports faster recovery of this tissue while helping ease the soreness and inflammation that come with it. It also improves lymphatic flow and helps release the tension behind a stiff neck, stiff shoulders and tension headaches.

When recovery is slow

INDIBA® is especially useful for injuries and wounds that haven't responded to other treatments, and may help improve chronic conditions. It supports the rehabilitation of both soft tissue and hard tissue.

Your questions answered

A little more
to know.

What is INDIBA®?

INDIBA® is a non-invasive medical and aesthetic technology that uses a controlled 448 kHz radiofrequency (RF) current to stimulate natural tissue repair, cellular metabolism and microcirculation. The tissue warms from within as the current moves through it, and the current also supports how cells function. There are no needles and nothing breaks the skin — your therapist simply glides a handpiece over the area.

What does it feel like?

A very comfortable warmth. You feel it spread through the area as the handpiece glides and your therapist works with their hands.

Which areas can be treated?

You choose. Tell your therapist which areas concern you — such as your neck, shoulders, back or knees — and the session focuses there. How you lie depends on the areas being treated.

Will I feel a difference after one session?

Many clients feel their aches ease after a session. For a specific concern, recovery builds over a course of sessions.

How often should I come?

For a specific concern: 12 sessions over 8 weeks. For maintenance: once a week.

Can it help with tension headaches?

It can help release the stiff neck and shoulders that often sit behind tension headaches. The deep warmth also helps you relax.

Is there anything I should avoid after a session?

No — there is nothing specific to avoid after a session.
