About the treatment

Meridian Bojin Facial is a facial at Elements Wellness in Singapore, where your therapist uses a handcrafted buffalo horn tool and Red Ginseng to apply focused pressure along facial meridian points, following principles from Traditional Chinese Medicine. It releases facial tension, supports circulation and sculpts your contours, giving an instant face lift.

Working along the face’s meridians

In TCM, meridians are channels that carry Qi — the body’s vital energy — and the points on your face are connected to your organs through them. Your therapist works along these points with a buffalo horn tool, and areas that feel tender or uncomfortable receive more attention to energise the channels.

In TCM, a well-balanced flow of energy rejuvenates cells and slows the ageing process, improving the complexion.

The Bojin technique

Bojin massage uses specially handcrafted buffalo horns to give an instant face lift. The gua sha-inspired tool applies acupressure along the meridian lines to relieve facial tension, improve blood flow and sculpt your contours, and the pressure on these points helps soften the appearance of fine lines.

The facial also includes a lymphatic massage of the face and shoulders, which aids relaxation and supports lymph flow.

Balancing Yin and Yang

TCM describes the body’s balance through Yin and Yang, two complementary forces. Working to regulate them helps moisten facial tissues and relieve tension.

Red ginseng alongside the technique

Premium red ginseng — used for energy balancing and for the look of the skin. It helps reduce the appearance of wrinkles and fine lines, supports a firmer look, brightens skin tone and helps protect the skin from free radicals (unstable molecules from sources such as pollution and sunlight that can stress skin cells) and environmental damage.

Your questions answered

What is a Bojin tool?

A Bojin tool is a handcrafted buffalo horn tool used in a gua sha-style massage. Your therapist uses it with pressure along the meridian points of your face to release tension and sculpt your contours.

What are meridians?

In Traditional Chinese Medicine, meridians are channels through which Qi, the body’s vital energy, is understood to flow. The points on your face connect to your organs through these channels, which is why the facial works on specific points rather than the whole face at once.

What will I notice after the facial?

You may notice your face looks lifted and fresher straight away, with less puffiness and a more radiant tone. Many clients also feel the tension in their face ease.

How often should I have the Meridian Bojin Facial?

For maintenance: once a month.

What does red ginseng do in this facial?

Premium red ginseng is used for energy balancing and for the look of your skin. It helps reduce the appearance of wrinkles and fine lines, supports a firmer look, brightens skin tone and helps protect the skin from free radicals and environmental damage.
