Why Vitamin C — and why fresh

Vitamin C does three things for your skin: it acts as an antioxidant, supports collagen, and brightens. The difficulty is that it is unstable and oxidises fast, so much of its potency can be lost before it reaches your skin.

This facial deals with that in two ways. It combines three advanced forms of Vitamin C, and each mono-dose is activated right before it is used, so your skin receives Vitamin C at its freshest.

Three forms of Vitamin C

AA2

AA2 (Ascorbic Acid 2-Glucoside) — a brightening form with sustained release, which supports a lasting glow.

SAP

SAP (Sodium Ascorbyl Phosphate) — supports the skin’s collagen and helps soften the look of fine lines, leaving skin smoother.

EAA

EAA (Ethyl Ascorbic Acid) — a stable, deep-penetrating form that helps protect skin from pollution and helps fade the look of dark spots.

Gentle exfoliation with Pronalen AHA 50

Exfoliation comes first, and it does two jobs. Pronalen AHA 50 is a blend of alpha hydroxy acids (AHAs) from passion fruit, lemon, grape and pineapple. It gently loosens the “glue” that holds dead skin cells together, so dull, dry and rough skin lifts away and smoother, brighter skin shows underneath.

Clearing that top layer also helps the freshly activated Vitamin C reach deeper and work more effectively.

Skin-loving ingredients

Alongside the Vitamin C, the facial uses a natural antimicrobial peptide that helps balance the skin’s pH and support its barrier, a deeply moisturising and conditioning ingredient that leaves skin soft and supple, and soothing ingredients such as Coolact that cool and calm the skin.

The oxygenating bubbling mask

The treatment finishes with an oxygenating bubbling mask. It foams up as it meets oxygen, creating a light, fluffy texture, and is enhanced with active ingredients to refresh the skin and support the results of the facial.

Your questions answered

What makes the 3C Vitamin Glow Treatment different?

It uses freshly activated, mono-dose Vitamin C, so the Vitamin C is opened and activated at your session rather than sitting in a bottle. It also combines three forms of Vitamin C (AA2, SAP and EAA) that each play a different role, then adds gentle AHA exfoliation and an oxygenating mask.

What does “freshly activated” mean?

Vitamin C oxidises quickly, which weakens it. A mono-dose is a single-use portion, and it is activated just before it goes on your skin, so you receive it at full potency.

What is Pronalen AHA 50?

Pronalen AHA 50 is a blend of alpha hydroxy acids (AHAs) from passion fruit, lemon, grape and pineapple. It loosens the bonds between dead skin cells so they lift away, which leaves smoother, brighter skin. Most clients find it comfortable.

Will my skin feel irritated afterwards?

Most clients feel comfortable afterwards. You may feel mild tingling during the treatment, which is normal, and the facial includes soothing ingredients such as Coolact to keep the experience comfortable.

How soon will I see results?

Many clients notice an immediate glow and smoother-looking skin after one session.

How often should I have the 3C Vitamin Glow Treatment?

For maintenance: once a month.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. The 3C Vitamin Glow Treatment can be paired with other treatments to address specific concerns. Ask your therapist to help you plan what suits your skin.

Is there any downtime?

There is no downtime. You can return to your day straight after your session.

How do I look after my skin between visits?

A consistent routine helps your skin keep its glow: a serum, a moisturiser, daily sunscreen and gentle exfoliation. Your session ends with Vitamin C Radiance Cream, and your therapist can recommend products for your skin type.
