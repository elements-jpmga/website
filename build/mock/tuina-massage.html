About the treatment

The massage at the heart of TCM

Tuina (推拿, pronounced “twee-nah”) is a traditional Chinese therapeutic massage rooted in Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM). Your therapist uses acupressure, kneading, rolling and rhythmic compression along your meridians and acupoints — combining meridian work with muscle work in one session.

Tuina has been practised in China for thousands of years. The name joins two words — tui, “to push”, and na, “to lift” — and those push-and-lift movements are how your therapist guides the flow of qi, the body’s energy in TCM. At Elements Wellness, this traditional method is combined with modern wellness techniques.

Working on muscles and meridians together

Tuina works on two levels at once. It releases tight muscles, and it also works on the meridians — the body’s energy channels in TCM — to clear blockages and restore harmony. It follows the same principles as acupuncture, but uses the therapist’s hands on your acupoints instead of needles.

02

Restoring balance

TCM sees comfort and health as a balance between yin and yang. When qi flows freely along the meridians, that balance holds; when the flow is blocked, you may feel it as stiffness, tension or tiredness. Every Tuina session works towards restoring that balance.

03

Tailored to what you need

Your therapist tailors each session to your needs — whether that is relieving tension, lifting low energy or focusing on a specific concern. Pressure on each acupoint and area varies in force, speed and duration, and your therapist may use one or several Tuina methods in the same session.

Your questions answered

What is Tuina massage?

Tuina is a TCM massage named after its two core movements — tui (to push) and na (to lift). Your therapist uses these push-and-lift movements, along with other manipulation techniques, to guide the flow of qi and help bring the body back into balance.

What techniques are used in Tuina?

Your therapist applies pressure to acupoints and areas of the body, varying the force, speed and duration. Tuina draws on eight basic techniques:

Pushing (tui)

Holding (na)

Palpating (mo)

Rejoining (jie)

Opposing (duan)

Lifting (ti)

Pressing (an)

Kneading (mo)

It also uses four specialised methods:

Rolling — a rolling movement over soft tissue, with gentler pressure for areas that feel strained or tight.

One-finger pushing — concentrated pressure through one finger on a single acupoint, used in TCM to work on the body’s internal balance.

Nei Gong — full-body manipulation that works with Nei Gong qi, energy that in TCM starts inside the body and flows outward, to help a depleted body feel restored.

Joint mobilisation — work around the joints, drawn from the bone-setting tradition, to ease stiffness and help them move more freely.

Your therapist may use one or several of these in a session, depending on your concern.

Is meridian massage the same as Tuina?

No — they share a focus on meridians and acupoints, but Tuina is broader. Tuina adds specialised manipulations, joint mobilisation and muscle work, while meridian massage mainly promotes the flow of qi, often with gentler strokes. If you would like meridian-focused work, Meridian Flush Therapy uses wooden tools along the meridians to ease soreness and a feeling of heaviness.

How often should I have Tuina?

For a specific concern: 5 sessions over 3 weeks.

For maintenance: once or twice a month.

How might I feel after Tuina?

Tuina is generally safe, though you may feel some discomfort during or after a session, particularly after stronger work. Slight bruising, tenderness or short-term soreness can follow, and usually settles on its own.

When is Tuina not suitable?

Tuina cannot cure every health problem. It may not suit — and may worsen — fevers, bone fractures, ligament tears and tumours. If you are prone to fractures or inflammation, or have open wounds, check with your doctor before booking.

Can I have Tuina during pregnancy?

Tuina is not recommended during pregnancy, because pressure on certain acupoints may affect the pregnancy. Prenatal Massage is a gentler option made for this stage.
