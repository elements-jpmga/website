About the treatment

Pregnancy aches,
and how massage helps

Pregnancy puts new strain on your back, hips, legs and neck, and it can make it hard to get comfortable or sleep well. Prenatal massage can help ease common discomforts such as back pain, hip pain, leg cramps and sciatic nerve pain by relieving tension in your muscles and joints.

Pregnancy can also reduce circulation in the legs and feet, because of increased pressure on blood vessels. Massage supports blood and lymphatic circulation, which helps ease swelling (oedema).

Prenatal massage can also help reduce stress hormones such as cortisol, the body’s main stress hormone, while supporting the release of feel-good hormones such as serotonin and dopamine. This can lift mood and emotional wellbeing.

Hormonal changes and increased blood volume during pregnancy can also cause sinus congestion. Massage techniques for the head, neck and shoulders can help ease tension headaches and sinus pressure.

Care that meets you where you are

Adapted for pregnancy

Your prenatal massage is performed by a qualified therapist. The techniques are adapted to the changes in your body — for example, you lie on your side with the support of pillows and cushions for comfort. Therapists use gentle, targeted techniques and avoid sensitive pressure points, for a relaxing and safe experience.

The massage is suitable from the 18th week of pregnancy, when the body is better prepared for massage therapy, and we see clients from their 18th to 32nd weeks. We keep a clean, soothing environment for every session.

Your questions answered

What is prenatal massage?

Prenatal massage, also called pre-partum massage, is a massage adapted for pregnancy. Your therapist adjusts the position and the techniques to suit your changing body, so you can rest and ease common aches in comfort.

Is prenatal massage safe?

Our therapists are qualified in prenatal care and use gentle, targeted techniques that avoid sensitive pressure points, which makes the session relaxing and safe within the weeks we treat, from the 18th to the 32nd week of pregnancy. Every pregnancy is different, so if you have any pregnancy-related conditions it is sensible to check with your healthcare provider first.

When can I have a prenatal massage?

We see pregnant clients from their 18th to 32nd weeks. The massage is suitable from the 18th week, when the body is better prepared for massage therapy.

Will it help with swelling?

Pregnancy can reduce circulation in the legs and feet because of increased pressure on blood vessels. Prenatal massage supports blood and lymphatic circulation, which helps ease swelling (oedema).

Can it help me sleep better?

Many mums-to-be find it hard to sleep because of discomfort, hormonal changes and anxiety. Prenatal massage promotes relaxation and eases tension, which can help you rest better and for longer.

Should I have a prenatal massage?

If you are experiencing discomfort or stress during pregnancy, a prenatal massage may offer relief and relaxation. Whether it is right for you depends on your health, any pregnancy-related conditions and how comfortable you feel with massage, so it helps to have your healthcare provider’s agreement first.
