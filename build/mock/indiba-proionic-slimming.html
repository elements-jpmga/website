About the treatment

How INDIBA®
works

448 kHz

Proionic® current

Technology and skilled touch

While traditional radiofrequency relies primarily on active thermal heat to boost collagen, INDIBA® adds a deeper physiological dimension. Powered by a patented 448 kHz Proionic® current, it supports cell function and faster tissue recovery, even with only gentle warmth. Adaptable for both face and body, each session works across superficial and deeper layers, with the focus set by the treatment you choose.

Working on stubborn fat

INDIBA® raises microcirculation and internal heat in the area treated. It reaches deeper, stubborn fatty tissue than most slimming technologies, helping reduce the look of stubborn fat and supporting the body's natural fat metabolism — without damaging surrounding cells. Improved lymphatic drainage helps clear what the tissue releases, along with excess fluid.

INDIBA®'s research suggests treated fat cells may become less active over time. This may help reduce fat deposits in the long term and limit new fat forming in treated areas.

Firmer, smoother skin

The internal heat stimulates collagen and elastin — the fibres that keep skin firm and elastic. As you lose volume, the skin over the area tightens, and cellulite and stretch marks look softer.

Supporting cell function

Better circulation improves nutrient exchange between cells and supports metabolism. INDIBA® helps cells recover their natural function, so the tissue works at its best.

Backed by clinical research

INDIBA® is a medically-backed technology, clinically assessed at medical centres. In INDIBA®'s assessments, regular treatments showed average results of 82% cellulite reduction, 87% skin tightening and 90% body shaping.

Your questions answered

to know.

What is INDIBA®?

INDIBA® is a non-invasive medical and aesthetic technology that uses a controlled 448 kHz radiofrequency (RF) current to stimulate natural tissue repair, cellular metabolism and microcirculation. The tissue warms from within as the current moves through it, and the current also supports how cells function. There are no needles and nothing breaks the skin — your therapist simply glides a handpiece over the area.

What does it feel like?

A comfortable warmth. The handpiece moves in firm but relaxing strokes, and you feel the warmth spread through the area.

Which areas can be treated?

Common areas are the tummy, love handles, upper arms, thighs and buttocks.

Can I treat more than one area in a visit?

Yes. Each area — your tummy, arms or thighs, for example — takes one 30-minute session, so book one session for each area you want treated.

How often should I come?

For a specific concern: 12 sessions over 8 weeks. For maintenance: once a week.
