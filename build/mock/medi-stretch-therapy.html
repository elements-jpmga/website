About the treatment

01

Why stretching on your own only goes so far

Self-stretching has limits. Some muscles cannot be stretched effectively on your own, and over time, poor positioning can strain the body rather than help it. Long hours at a desk, hard training or physical work leave muscles tight — often in places a stretch you do yourself never quite reaches.

02

Your therapist does the stretching

In Medi-Stretch® Therapy, you don’t stretch yourself. Your therapist moves your body for you and stabilises it in proper alignment, so each stretch can go further while staying controlled. That support is what lets you stretch deeper without straining.

03

Working with your stretch reflex

Your muscles have a built-in defence against injury — the stretch reflex. If a muscle is lengthened too far or too quickly, it contracts to protect itself, which is why forcing a stretch tends to backfire. Your therapist watches for this response and lengthens each muscle gradually, so it has time to release rather than tighten.

04

You set the pace

You tell your therapist how each stretch feels, and you remain in control of the session throughout. The depth of every stretch follows your comfort level.

Your questions answered

What is Medi-Stretch® Therapy?

It is assisted stretching performed for you by a certified therapist. Rather than stretching yourself, your therapist moves your body through each stretch, holds it in proper alignment and lengthens each muscle gradually.

How is it different from stretching on my own?

Your therapist can reach muscles that are hard to stretch effectively by yourself, and keeps your body aligned while doing it. Self-stretching often involves poor positioning, which can strain the body over time. With support, you can stretch further without straining.

What is the stretch reflex?

It is your body’s protective response to being stretched too far or too fast — the muscle contracts to guard itself. Your therapist watches for this response and lengthens each muscle gradually, so it releases instead of tightening against the stretch.

Will it hurt?

Each stretch follows your comfort level. You tell your therapist how it feels as you go, and you stay in control of the session throughout.

Is it better before or after a workout?

It suits both. Before training, it loosens tight muscles; afterwards, it helps reduce soreness and supports recovery.

How often should I come?

For a specific concern: 5 sessions over 3 weeks. For maintenance: once a week.
