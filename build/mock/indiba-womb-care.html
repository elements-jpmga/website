About the treatment

INDIBA® Womb Care is a non-invasive treatment for women that works across the abdomen with INDIBA® radiofrequency. Gentle warmth reaches the tissue around the uterus and ovaries, supporting cell function and blood flow where you need it most. It is also used after childbirth, to help restore the uterus and bring tone back to the tummy.

448 kHz · Proionic®

How INDIBA® works

While traditional radiofrequency relies primarily on active thermal heat to boost collagen, INDIBA® adds a deeper physiological dimension. Powered by a patented 448 kHz Proionic® current, it supports cell function and faster tissue recovery, even with only gentle warmth. Adaptable for both face and body, each session works across superficial and deeper layers, with the focus set by the treatment you choose.

Warmth from within

INDIBA® raises the temperature of the tissue from the inside, not just the skin. That warmth increases blood and oxygen flow to the womb and ovaries — which may help ease cramping and the cold, chilly feeling some women notice in the lower abdomen. It is a comfortable, non-invasive way to bring deep warmth to an area that is hard to reach.

Supporting the womb

The 448 kHz current supports how cells in the tissue function, including those of the uterus. As circulation improves, so does nutrient exchange at cell level, helping strengthen the womb and support the ovaries. Lymphatic flow increases too, helping clear excess fluid that collects around the tummy.

A firmer-looking abdomen

The same warmth activates metabolism in the area and supports the production of collagen and elastin — the fibres that keep skin firm. With regular sessions, loose tummy skin can look tighter and cellulite less noticeable. This is why many clients choose INDIBA® for the abdomen as much as for monthly comfort.

After childbirth

INDIBA® Womb Care is used as postnatal care. Warmth and better circulation help restore the uterus after birth, while collagen support helps the stretched tummy regain firmness.

Your questions answered

What is INDIBA®?

INDIBA® is a non-invasive medical and aesthetic technology that uses a controlled 448 kHz radiofrequency (RF) current to stimulate natural tissue repair, cellular metabolism and microcirculation. The tissue warms from within as the current moves through it, and the current also supports how cells function. There are no needles and nothing breaks the skin — your therapist simply glides a handpiece over the area.

What does it feel like?

Comfortable warmth. You feel the heat spread across your abdomen as the handpiece glides, and most clients find it relaxing rather than intense.

Can I have it during my period?

No. Please book your session for a day outside your period.

Can I have it after giving birth?

Yes — INDIBA® Womb Care is used as postnatal care, to help restore the uterus and firm the tummy. Let us know when you gave birth when you book.

How often should I come?

For maintenance: once or twice a month.

Can I add anything to my session?

Yes. The Volcanic Mud Mask works well as an add-on and is applied after the INDIBA® treatment. See Massage & Recovery Boosters for its added time and price.
