About this treatment

What is fascia?

Fascia is the connective tissue that surrounds your muscles and other structures throughout the body. When it feels tight or restricted, you may notice stiffness, a pulling sensation or less freedom of movement.

A focused approach to release

Your therapist uses specialised gold- or silver-plated instruments to apply precise, controlled strokes along areas of restriction. This tool-assisted work helps ease tightness and supports fascia mobility.

Targeted to your body

The treatment focuses on where you feel restricted, rather than following a fixed full-body routine. Your areas of tension guide the session.

Pressure that follows your comfort

The work can feel more intense than a relaxation massage, especially in tighter areas. Pressure is controlled and adjusted to your comfort level.

Your questions answered

How is Fascia Release Therapy different from a regular massage?

It is targeted tool-assisted work on areas of fascial restriction. Rather than a fixed full-body routine, the session focuses on the places where you feel tight or restricted.

Does it feel painful?

It can feel more intense than a typical relaxation massage, especially in restricted areas. Pressure is controlled and adjusted to your comfort. Tell your therapist how it feels throughout the session.

What tools are used?

Your therapist uses specialised gold- or silver-plated instruments to apply precise, controlled strokes along targeted areas.

How often should I come?

For a specific concern: 12 sessions over 8 weeks.
For maintenance: once a week.

How long is the session, and what does it cost?
