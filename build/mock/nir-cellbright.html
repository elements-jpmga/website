About the treatment

Warmth. Cooling.
A brighter, firmer look.

The NIR CellBright Facial uses near-infrared light to warm the deeper layers of your skin, where collagen is made, then follows with Cryo-Infusion cooling to refresh the skin and hold in hydration. Skin can look brighter and feel tighter straight away, and the benefits keep building over the following weeks. There are no needles and no downtime.

01 / NEAR-INFRARED

Near-infrared warmth: working below the surface

Most facials work on the surface. Near-infrared light, delivered with Alma™ technology, passes through the surface to warm the dermis, the deeper layer of skin where collagen is made, to between about 39°C and 42°C. It feels like a comfortable warmth, and the handpiece keeps moving across the skin so the heat builds evenly.

As the tissue warms, two things happen. The collagen fibres already there contract, which can give skin a tighter, more lifted look straight away. The warmth also stimulates your skin's collagen-making cells, called fibroblasts, and boosts circulation, supporting your skin's own collagen renewal over the weeks that follow. Circulation is also what gives the complexion its brighter, lit-from-within look.

Gentle warmth may also help relax the superficial fascia, the thin connective layer under the skin, and the tension held in the jaw. Both can contribute to a heavier-looking lower face.

02 / CRYO-INFUSION

Cryo-Infusion cooling: the contrast that resets the skin

Warmth is followed by cold. Cooling helps reduce visible puffiness, leaves skin feeling tighter and refreshed, helps refine the look of pores and holds in hydration for a dewy finish.

Why the two work together

Warmth activates the deeper collagen response, and cold calms and sets the result at the surface. Together they give a visible lift and glow now, while supporting firmer, more defined-looking skin over time.

Your questions answered

How is this different from a regular facial?

A regular facial works mainly on cleansing, hydration and the condition of the skin's surface. The NIR CellBright Facial goes deeper: near-infrared light warms the layer beneath the surface where collagen is made, then cooling refreshes the skin and reduces puffiness. The result is brighter, firmer-looking skin, with benefits that keep building over time.

What is collagen, and why does warming the skin matter?

Collagen is the protein that gives skin its structure and firmness, and it is made by cells called fibroblasts in the dermis. As production slows, skin can lose firmness and the jawline and cheeks can look softer. Warmth prompts the collagen already there to tighten and stimulates fibroblasts to make more. New collagen takes time to mature, which is why firming builds gradually over the weeks after your session rather than all at once.

What is Cryo-Infusion?

Cryo-Infusion is the cooling step that follows the near-infrared warmth. The skin is cooled rapidly, which narrows the blood vessels near the surface, a deliberate contrast with the warmth before it that supports healthy circulation through the skin. The cooling helps de-puff the skin and leaves it feeling tighter and refreshed.

What does it feel like?

A comfortable, relaxing warmth, similar to a hot stone massage or warm sunlight on the skin. The Cryo-Infusion then brings a cooling contrast.

Is there any downtime?

No. There are no needles and no downtime, so you can apply makeup and carry on with your day straight after your facial.

How often should I have it?

For maintenance: once a month. The benefits build the more consistently you return.

Is it suitable for men?

Yes. The treatment works on the same skin layers whoever is in the chair, and the concerns it addresses (a less defined jawline, larger-looking pores, tired-looking skin) are not specific to any gender.
