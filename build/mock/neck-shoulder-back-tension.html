The small things add up

When everyday tension starts to build

Tension does not always follow a hard workout or a long day at your desk. Small habits can quietly add up — even when you do not feel physically busy.

01

Concentrating deeply

You may clench your jaw or lift your shoulders while working, driving or focusing on a task, without noticing.

02

Staying still

Reading, scrolling or watching a show can keep the same muscles working in one position for longer than you realise.

03

Carrying everyday essentials

A laptop bag on one shoulder, heavy shopping bags or carrying a child on the same hip can place repeated demands on particular areas.

04

Trying to sit perfectly straight

Holding yourself rigidly upright can become tiring too. Comfortable posture includes room to move and change position.

Over time, you might notice heavy shoulders, tightness between the shoulder blades or a back that feels stiff when you finally get up.

Understanding your body

What are muscle knots?

A muscle knot is a tight, tender spot in a muscle that has not fully relaxed. You may feel it as a small lump or a firm band under the skin — often across the top of the shoulders, between the shoulder blades or in the lower back.

Knots can form when a muscle works in the same position for long periods, or is pushed harder than usual, and part of it stays tight instead of releasing. They may feel tender when pressed, ache into nearby areas or make it uncomfortable to turn your head or roll your shoulders.

Focused pressure can help ease a knot that does not release on its own. Deep Tissue Massage and Ginseng Bojin Meridian Therapy both offer concentrated work on stubborn knots.

Tech Neck & Headaches

Screen time brings several of these habits together: a head tilted towards a phone, shoulders held in place and long stretches without much movement. The tension may become more noticeable around the neck, upper back and base of the skull as the day goes on.

Frequently asked questions

Which treatment should I choose for neck and shoulder tension?

Deep Tissue Massage is a suitable starting point if you enjoy firm pressure. Tuina offers a traditional Chinese massage approach, while Ginseng Bojin provides focused tool-assisted work. If tension is concentrated around your head and neck, consider Myofascial Head / Neck Therapy.

What if my back feels tight after exercise?

Sports Massage focuses on muscles used during training and helps ease exercise-related soreness and tightness. Medi-Stretch® Therapy may suit you if tight muscles and flexibility are your main concerns.

What is the difference between massage and fascia release?

Massage primarily works on muscle tension through hands-on pressure and movement. Fascia Release Therapy uses specialised tools to work on the connective tissue surrounding muscles, helping ease tightness and restriction.

Does deeper pressure mean better results?

The most suitable pressure depends on your comfort and the treatment approach. Tell your therapist what feels manageable and speak up if the work feels too intense.

Can I book without knowing which treatment to choose?

Yes. Share where you feel tension, whether it follows work or exercise, and whether you prefer massage, stretching or targeted therapy. Our team can help you decide where to begin.
