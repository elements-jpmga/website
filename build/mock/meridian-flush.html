About the treatment

When your body feels sore and heavy

Meridian Flush Therapy is a deep, hands-on therapy rooted in Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM). Your therapist works specially grooved wooden sticks along your meridian channels in firm, flushing strokes, using a plant-based essence containing mugwort to support the work.

Muscles that are overworked or held tense for long periods can harden, ache and feel stiff to move. Alongside that, your body may feel heavy, puffy or sluggish — a state TCM links to stagnation in the body’s channels. Meridian Flush Therapy works on both.

The technique

Grooved wooden sticks

The sticks are specially grooved and shaped to fit the areas most prone to aches. Drawn along the channels in a flushing action, they apply even pressure that comfortably loosens hardened muscle. Scraped across the muscles, they cover larger areas and help move lymph.

Mugwort essence

Mugwort is a warming herb in TCM, traditionally used to support circulation. A plant-based essence containing mugwort is used during the treatment to boost the effects of the stick work.

Meridians and qi

In TCM, meridians are the channels through which qi, the body’s energy, flows. Your therapist “flushes” along these channels on your neck, shoulders, back and legs in a systematic sequence. The work supports qi flow, in TCM terms, while improving circulation and encouraging lymph flow at the same time.

Fascia release

The pressure reaches into fascia, the connective tissue around your muscles, and into muscle adhesions — places where tissue has become stuck together. Working these areas helps ease tension, soreness and stiffness.

Easing dampness

Dampness is a TCM concept describing fluid that has stagnated in the body. You may notice it as heaviness, puffy skin, water retention or sluggish energy. By encouraging circulation and lymph flow, the flush helps ease that heavy, waterlogged feeling.

Your questions answered

What is a meridian flush?

In TCM, meridians are the channels through which qi flows. A meridian flush works along these channels in a set sequence, using grooved wooden sticks to apply even pressure. It supports qi flow and circulation, in TCM terms, and helps ease tension, soreness and heaviness.

What does it feel like?

The pressure is firm and can feel intense, much like deep tissue work. As the hardened muscle loosens, it is usually followed by a relaxed sense of release.

What is dampness in TCM?

Dampness is a TCM concept describing fluid that has stagnated in the body. It tends to show up as heaviness, puffy skin, water retention and sluggish energy. Meridian Flush Therapy helps ease these by encouraging circulation and lymph flow.

Why is mugwort used?

Mugwort is a warming herb in TCM, traditionally used to support circulation. The plant-based essence containing mugwort boosts the effects of the stick work.

How often should I come?

For a specific concern: 5 sessions over 3 weeks.

For maintenance: once or twice a month.

Will it leave marks?

The stick work can leave your skin looking a little red in the areas that have been worked. This redness is temporary and fades on its own.

How is it different from Fascia Release Therapy?

Both use tools rather than hands alone. Meridian Flush Therapy follows the TCM meridian channels across your neck, shoulders, back and legs, and suits soreness, stiffness and heaviness. Fascia Release Therapy focuses its tool work on restricted fascia and ease of movement.
